Wildfire (WUI) rules

Keep 5-ft noncombustible Ember Ignition Zone around structures.

Wildfire zone: Over half of Austin is in a WUI zone (A, B or C). Check your address on the 2024 WUI Code map. WUI projects need residential plan review, exact product specs on plans, and two WUI inspections. Applies to permit applications on/after July 10, 2025; existing homes are not required to retrofit.
